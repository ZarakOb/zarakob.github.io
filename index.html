Tracer MR

A standalone mixed-reality app that places transparent artwork over real paper, canvas or walls so you can trace it by hand through passthrough.

UnityMeta XR SDKMRUKOpenXRPassthroughHand tracking
Challenge

Tracing only works if the image stays locked to the real surface while your head and hands move. Any drift and the pen misses the line.

Approach

Prototyped spatial anchoring first, before building any features, and tested stability on-device until it was tight enough to trace against. Then built trace-mode controls and an in-headset floating menu.

Result

Stable anchored overlays on real surfaces, a curated line-art library, and custom image import with background removal and outline extraction. Now in launch prep.

Location-based AR · iOS & Android · Sole developer at Bave (client project)

Quest Clues

A city-wide AR mystery game. Players walk to real places like universities, parks, landmarks and malls, collect clues in AR, and piece together a story.

UnityAR FoundationARCoreMapboxGPSFirebaseREST APIsIAP
Challenge

The game had to work in practically any city, not just one hand-designed map.

Approach

Built the experience around categories of points of interest that exist in every city, and placed clues and story beats dynamically around them based on the player's location.

Result
  • Google, Apple, phone and email login
  • 9 languages
  • Web dashboard for adding new stories in a few clicks, live without an app update
  • In-app purchases; submitted to the App Store and Google Play
VR · Meta Quest · Founder, led a team of 3

Mind Over Fear

A VR platform designed for exposure therapy, putting people in controlled, repeatable versions of the situations they fear.

Unity 6Meta Quest SDKOpenXRXR Interaction ToolkitURPFirebase
Challenge

This is a medical product. A scenario that feels wrong or escalates badly doesn't just fail, it can set a patient back. Every environment had to be built with extra care.

Approach

Built separate scenarios for claustrophobia (elevator, MRI scanner), road crossing, and social and public-speaking settings with an AI-driven audience, then put them in front of licensed therapists and clinical professionals for validation.

Result

Validated with clinicians, incubated at the National Incubation Center and Orbit Startups, and presented to doctors, healthcare stakeholders and investors.
